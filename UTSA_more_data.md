## Supplemental Datasets - UTSA Datathon

**Homes in FEMA Flood Zones by County in Six States, 2026** \
https://huggingface.co/datasets/DreamyLeadsResearch/flood-zone-homes-by-county-2026

**Flood Sentinel** \
https://huggingface.co/datasets/Ymx1025/floodsentinel
Frozen gauge, warning, and reference corpora for historical U.S. county flood events, with the results of every acquisition policy evaluated on them. The data support the manuscript FloodSentinel: An Agentic AI Framework for Active Information Acquisition and Evidence Governance in Flood Monitoring (Xiao, Murugan, Brody, and Mostafavi; submitted to Computers, Environment and Urban Systems, 2026). Code: https://github.com/YimingXiao98/floodsentinel.

**Global Database for Comprehensive Disaster Data**  \
https://public.emdat.be/
Over 27,000 disaster records from 1900 to the present day. Data sourced from diverse organizations: UN agencies, NGOs, insurance companies, research institutes, and press agencies. Available in open access for non-commercial use.
Managed and distributed by the Centre for Research on the Epidemiology of Disasters (CRED).

**San Antonio Pilot Street Light Sensor Data**  \
https://data.sanantonio.gov/dataset/street-light-sensor-data 
The SmartSA Smart Streetlight program pilots remote lighting controls, and five environmental sensor use-cases on up to 40 streetlights in each of the three Innovation Zones: Downtown, Brooks and the Medical Center for a total of up to 120 streetlights. 
Data included: Flood levels sensing, Air quality sensing, Temperature sensing, Ambient noise detection, Parking availability sensing

**Texas Water Districts Map** (Updated Nov 2026)  \
https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Texas-Commission-on-Environmental-Quality-Texas-Wa/ruhk-kxgs
TCEQ provides easily accessible Texas Water District data to the public and to regional water planning groups. Find information on municipal utility districts, special utility districts, river authorities, water systems, water control and improvement districts, and other information. Districts include their business contact, office address, and associated county.

**TCEQ Water Quality Individual Permits Active/Pending** (Updated Nov 2026)  \
https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Texas-Commission-on-Environmental-Quality-Water-Qu/7fq8-wig2/about_data
This dataset includes basic information about the stormwater and wastewater individual permits administered by the TCEQ Water Quality Division.

**Public Utility Commission of Texas - Informal Complaints**  \
https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Public-Utility-Commission-of-Texas-Informal-Compla/cxnx-7tf4/about_data
This dataset includes the electric, telephone, water or sewer complaints filed with the Consumer Protection Division.

**TCEQ Sanitary Sewer Overflows**  \
https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Texas-Commission-on-Environmental-Quality-Sanitary/8kc5-95uk/about_data
Dataset of unauthorized discharge of untreated or partially treated wastewater from a collection system or its components (e.g., a manhole, lift station, or cleanout) before it reaches a wastewater treatment facility. This can occur if there is significant inflow/infiltration, the collection system is poorly operated and maintained, or the system lacks adequate capacity to collect or store flows for treatment, or to treat them.

**September 2002 Post-Tropical Storm Fay Photos on the Texas Coast**  \
https://www.glo.texas.gov/land/gis-maps-and-data/aerials/aerial-imagery-lidar-data-2000-2009/september-2002-obliques-post-tropical-storm-fay-photos-texas-coast 

**TCEQ Complaints** \
https://data.texas.gov/dataset/Texas-Commission-on-Environmental-Quality-Complain/tdzs-qqjn/about_data
View complaints received by the TCEQ with their associated Complaint Number. The information alleged by the complainant is documented and associated to that unique number and then further investigated. Note, a complaint may be listed more than once if there are multiple associated Customer Names, Released Materials, Media, and/or Effects.

#### More Datasets: San Antonio Stormwater Infrastructure

**IMP Drainage**  \
https://data.sanantonio.gov/dataset/imp-drainage
The Infrastructure Management Program (IMP) is a five-year rolling program which focuses on the maintenance of San Antonio’s Infrastructure. Service needs are identified city wide and are scheduled for street maintenance, alley maintenance, drainage maintenance, sidewalks, traffic signals, pavement markings and Advanced Transportation District (ATD) related projects. Drainage maintenance addresses debris removal within our Natural Creeks and restores the shape of constructed channels to ensure proper water flow.

**Stormwater Underground**  \
https://opendata-cosagis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/CoSAGIS::stormwaterunderground/about
This geographical polyline dataset is used to assist the Storm Water Engineering Department with locating drainage systems within San Antonio, Texas.

**Storm Inlets**  \
https://opendata-cosagis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/CoSAGIS::storminlets/about
This is a geographical point dataset depicting the Inlet location and infrastructure within San Antonio, Texas.

**Manholes**  \
https://opendata-cosagis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/CoSAGIS::manholes/about
This feature class identifies all of the Stormwater Manholes in the city of San Antonio, TX. It also includes information on the diameter, depth, & ID of each manhole. 

**Storm Channels**  \
https://opendata-cosagis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/CoSAGIS::stormchannels/about
This is a geographical linear dataset depicting the channel locations and infrastructure within San Antonio, Texas.

**Outfalls**  \
https://opendata-cosagis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/CoSAGIS::outfalls/about 
Outlet Components are noteworthy transitional features of stormwater infrastructure that have inspection and maintenance significance. 

**Low Water Crossings**  \
https://opendata-cosagis.opendata.arcgis.com/datasets/4089adb69af14608acfb02703b1dd439_0/about
This is a graphical point dataset depicting the Low Water Crossings that are within the boundaries of the Federal Emergency Management Agency's (FEMA) Digital Flood Insurance Rate Map (DFIRM) floodplain area in Bexar County, Texas. This dataset was modified by the COSA Public Works Department, Storm Water Engineering Division to identify Low Water Crossings.
